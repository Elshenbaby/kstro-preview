// 05 — FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

How long does a website take? +

It depends on the package. Each package has a set timeline that starts once the deposit and your questionnaire are in.

How do payments work? +

50% to start, 50% when the site is ready to launch. We accept InstaPay, bank transfer, Vodafone Cash, card and cash.

What do I need to prepare? +

Your logo, text and photos if you have them. Our questionnaire walks you through everything else.

Can the site be in Arabic and English? +

Yes. We design for both from the start, including right-to-left layouts.

Who owns the website? +

You do. When the project is delivered and paid, we hand over the domain, hosting and every admin login.

What happens after launch? +

Our support team handles fixes and questions, and we can keep improving the site with you.

Have a projectin mind?

Answer a few quick questions. It takes about two minutes, and someone from our team will contact you.
